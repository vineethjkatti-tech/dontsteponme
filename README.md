## Description

**don't-step-on-me** is a **survival-based game** where you are a **bug** and you** escape the shoes falling from above!!**

## How to Play!!

Use keys **A** and **D** to **scurry** and press **Space** to **Curl up**.

Use the stool that **occurs periodically** and **breaks** with just one crush to protect yourself...

You may take is easy at first, but beware!! **the difficulty advance with time played!!**

When you are **curl** under a shoe you **goooo rollingg** in the opposite direction!!

## Features!!

1. In this game the **difficulty increases** as you **play for longer**. This includes the **shadow duration** (_decreases_), **the time for which the shoe lingers** (_increases_), **the amount of shoes falling at a time** (_increases_), **the amount of times for which the shoe takes your location to fall** (_increases_).

2. The bug can go under the **stool**, which **spawns occasionally** at **random places**, to **protect** itself from the **shoe impact**. But, keep in mind that the shoe can only protect you from **one hit!**
   
3. The bug can **curl** to protect itself from a **hit** or **multiple hits** cause the curl duration lasts for about **2 seconds**.

4. If the bug does **curl**, and a shoe does happen to **fall on it**, the bug **pinballs** or **rolls rapidly** **opposite** to the **area the shoe had fallen on it**. If the shoe falls on the bug **dead center** then the bug chooses a **random direction** to pinball.

5. On the top edge of the screen, this game also features a **HUD** which contains your play time and high score.

6. The high score timer records your highest score **from the time you have started to play it** (_on itch browser or downloaded_) and **retains it till you reinstall the game or switch browser accounts.**

7. The HUD also features a **cooldown timer** for the **curl time**.

8. All the buttons in this game have a **cursor animation** which adds to the **arcade/retro** style of the game.

9. There are **sound SFXs** for several functions in the game which include the **shoe stomping**, **curl cooldown**, **curl**, **chair break** and **bug smashing**.

10. Apart from a strong stomp sound when a **shoe falls**, there is also a **screen shake** which sells the stomp and is **quite satisfying** I feel!!

11. There are also **tween** and **flash effects** for many functions, like **curl reload** (_Cooldown HUD scales and reverts_), **Bug uncurl**, **Bug bounce on wall** (_while pinballing_) which are added to provide **attention to detail** and **user satisfaction**. _Make sure to check them out!!_
    
12. There is also a **near miss** function where if the **bug misses getting squashed** by the shoe while being near to the shoe, the bug gets a **speed boost for about 1.5s** and a **"close call!" popup appears and fades** above the bug. The speed boost is proportional to how close the miss was!! (_it will get more speed boost if it misses the shoe by less distance_).

13. There is also **sound toggle** in pause menu where you can toggle **music** and **sfx** on and off!!
