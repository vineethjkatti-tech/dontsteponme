A game where you are "**just a numb little bug that's gotta survive**" from huge shoes that rain on you!!

## Description

This is a survival game where you are a bug and you have to avoid getting crushed beneath the merciless foot of a human!

Use keys **A** and **D** to **scurry** and press **Space** to **Curl up**.

Use the stool that **occurs periodically** and **breaks** with just one crush to protect yourself...

You may take is easy at first, but beware!! **the difficulty advance with time played!!**
